# Project Charter

## Project Title

**Cambodia Banking Risk & Business Intelligence Platform**

---

## Project Overview

The Cambodia Banking Risk & Business Intelligence Platform is an end-to-end analytics project designed to simulate the operations of a medium-sized commercial bank in Cambodia. The project aims to provide business, operational, customer, and risk insights through a centralized data warehouse, analytical reporting, and interactive dashboards.

The platform will integrate business intelligence, banking analytics, and risk management concepts to support data-driven decision-making. Synthetic banking data will be generated using realistic assumptions based on Cambodia's banking environment, customer demographics, loan products, and economic conditions.

---

## Background

Commercial banks rely on accurate data and timely reporting to monitor business performance, customer growth, profitability, and risk exposure. However, decision-makers often face challenges when information is fragmented across multiple systems.

To address this challenge, this project will simulate a banking analytics environment that consolidates customer, account, loan, transaction, and risk data into a centralized platform for analysis and reporting.

---

## Project Objectives

### Business Objectives

* Monitor business performance across branches and products.
* Evaluate customer growth and engagement.
* Track loan portfolio performance.
* Support strategic decision-making through KPI monitoring.
* Improve visibility into risk exposure and portfolio quality.

### Analytical Objectives

* Design a banking data warehouse.
* Build realistic synthetic banking datasets.
* Develop SQL-based analytical reporting.
* Create interactive Power BI dashboards.
* Generate business and risk insights.
* Support future forecasting and predictive analytics.
