# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project Title

**Cambodia Banking Risk & Business Intelligence Platform**

---

# 1. Introduction

## Purpose

This document defines the business requirements, stakeholder needs, analytical objectives, and business questions for the Cambodia Banking Risk & Business Intelligence Platform.

The purpose of the platform is to provide management, branch operations, and risk teams with data-driven insights regarding business performance, customer behavior, portfolio quality, and risk exposure.

---

# 2. Business Problem Statement

Modern banks generate large volumes of customer, account, transaction, and loan data. However, decision-makers often struggle to consolidate this information into actionable insights.

Without an integrated reporting and analytics platform, management may face challenges such as:

* Limited visibility into business performance.
* Difficulty monitoring loan portfolio quality.
* Inconsistent KPI reporting.
* Delayed identification of emerging risks.
* Limited understanding of customer behavior.
* Challenges in evaluating branch performance.

The bank requires a centralized Business Intelligence and Risk Analytics platform to support strategic decision-making.

# 6. Functional Requirements

## FR-01 Data Storage

The platform shall store customer, account, transaction, loan, payment, and credit profile information.

---

## FR-02 KPI Monitoring

The platform shall calculate and display key banking KPIs.

---

## FR-03 Risk Monitoring

The platform shall monitor delinquency, default rates, and NPL ratios.

---

## FR-04 Customer Analytics

The platform shall support customer segmentation and retention analysis.

---

## FR-05 Branch Analytics

The platform shall support branch-level performance monitoring.

---

## FR-06 Historical Analysis

The platform shall maintain historical data from 2021 to 2025.

---

## FR-07 Dashboard Reporting

The platform shall provide interactive Power BI dashboards for business and risk monitoring.

---

# 7. Non-Functional Requirements

## Performance

The platform should efficiently process large volumes of synthetic banking data.

---

## Scalability

The platform should support future expansion of customers, transactions, and products.

---

## Maintainability

Data generation and reporting processes should be modular and easy to update.

---

## Usability

Dashboards should be intuitive and easy for business users to understand.

# 8. Expected Business Value

The platform is expected to:

* Improve visibility into banking operations.
* Enable data-driven decision-making.
* Strengthen risk monitoring capabilities.
* Improve customer understanding.
* Support strategic planning and growth initiatives.
* Demonstrate practical banking analytics and business intelligence capabilities.

---

# 9. Business Requirements Summary

The Banking Risk & Business Intelligence Platform will serve as a centralized analytical environment that integrates business performance monitoring, customer analytics, branch performance evaluation, and credit risk management.

The platform will provide stakeholders with actionable insights through KPI monitoring, historical analysis, and interactive business intelligence dashboards, enabling informed decision-making across the organization.
