# KPI Framework

## Project Title

**Cambodia Banking Risk & Business Intelligence Platform**

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) used to monitor business performance, customer behavior, branch performance, and credit risk within the Banking Risk & Business Intelligence Platform.

The KPI framework provides a standardized measurement system to support decision-making for management, risk teams, and operational stakeholders.

---

# 2. KPI Categories

The platform organizes KPIs into four categories:

1. Business Performance KPIs
2. Customer Analytics KPIs
3. Risk Management KPIs
4. Branch Performance KPIs

---

# 3. Business Performance KPIs

## KPI-BP-01: Total Customers

### Definition

Total number of registered customers.

### Formula

```sql
COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id)
```

### Business Purpose

Measures overall customer base growth.

### Stakeholders

* Executive Management
* Branch Managers

---

## KPI-BP-02: Total Deposits

### Definition

Total deposit balances held by customers.

### Formula

```sql
SUM(current_balance)
```

### Business Purpose

Measures funding strength and liquidity.

### Stakeholders

* Executive Management
* Finance Team

---

## KPI-BP-03: Total Loans Outstanding

### Definition

Total active loan balances currently held by customers.

### Formula

```sql
SUM(outstanding_balance)
```

### Business Purpose

Measures lending activity and portfolio size.

### Stakeholders

* Executive Management
* Risk Management

---

## KPI-BP-04: Deposit Growth Rate

### Definition

Percentage increase or decrease in deposits compared to the previous period.

### Formula

```text
(Current Deposits - Previous Deposits)
/
Previous Deposits × 100
```

### Business Purpose

Evaluates business growth and funding expansion.

---

## KPI-BP-05: Loan Growth Rate

### Definition

Percentage increase or decrease in loan portfolio size.

### Formula

```text
(Current Loans - Previous Loans)
/
Previous Loans × 100
```

### Business Purpose

Measures lending growth.

---

## KPI-BP-06: Revenue

### Definition

Revenue generated from lending activities.

### Formula

```text
Interest Income
+
Fee Income
```

### Business Purpose

Measures overall business performance.

---

# 4. Customer Analytics KPIs

## KPI-CA-01: Active Customers

### Definition

Customers with at least one transaction within the last 90 days.

### Formula

```sql
COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id)
```

Filtered by recent transaction activity.

### Business Purpose

Measures customer engagement.

---

## KPI-CA-02: New Customer Acquisition

### Definition

Number of customers joining during a reporting period.

### Formula

```sql
COUNT(customer_id)
```

Where join_date falls within the selected period.

### Business Purpose

Measures customer growth.

---

## KPI-CA-03: Customer Retention Rate

### Definition

Percentage of customers retained during a reporting period.

### Formula

```text
Retained Customers
/
Customers at Beginning of Period × 100
```

### Business Purpose

Measures customer loyalty.

---

## KPI-CA-04: Product Adoption Rate

### Definition

Percentage of customers using a specific product.

### Formula

```text
Customers Using Product
/
Total Customers × 100
```

### Business Purpose

Measures product penetration.

---

## KPI-CA-05: Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

### Definition

Estimated value generated by a customer throughout their relationship with the bank.

### Formula

```text
Total Revenue Generated Per Customer
```

### Business Purpose

Identifies high-value customer segments.

---

# 5. Risk Management KPIs

## KPI-RM-01: Default Rate

### Definition

Percentage of loans that enter default status.

### Formula

```text
Defaulted Loans
/
Total Loans × 100
```

### Business Purpose

Measures credit portfolio quality.

---

## KPI-RM-02: Non-Performing Loan Ratio (NPL Ratio)

### Definition

Percentage of loan portfolio classified as non-performing.

### Formula

```text
Non-Performing Loans
/
Total Loan Portfolio × 100
```

### Business Purpose

Primary indicator of loan portfolio health.

### Stakeholders

* Risk Management
* Executive Management

---

## KPI-RM-03: Delinquency Rate

### Definition

Percentage of loans past due.

### Formula

```text
Past Due Loans
/
Total Loans × 100
```

### Business Purpose

Provides early warning signals before defaults occur.

---

## KPI-RM-04: Average Credit Score

### Definition

Average customer credit score.

### Formula

```sql
AVG(credit_score)
```

### Business Purpose

Measures portfolio quality.

---

## KPI-RM-05: Exposure at Risk (EAR)

### Definition

Outstanding balance associated with high-risk customers.

### Formula

```sql
SUM(outstanding_balance)
```

For customers classified as High Risk or Very High Risk.

### Business Purpose

Estimates potential future losses.

---

## KPI-RM-06: Risk Distribution

### Definition

Distribution of customers across risk categories.

### Categories

* Very Low Risk
* Low Risk
* Medium Risk
* High Risk
* Very High Risk

### Business Purpose

Monitors portfolio composition.

---

# 6. Branch Performance KPIs

## KPI-BR-01: Revenue by Branch

### Definition

Revenue generated by each branch.

### Formula

```sql
SUM(revenue)
GROUP BY branch
```

### Business Purpose

Measures branch productivity.

---

## KPI-BR-02: Loan Portfolio by Branch

### Definition

Total outstanding loans by branch.

### Formula

```sql
SUM(outstanding_balance)
GROUP BY branch
```

### Business Purpose

Measures lending volume.

---

## KPI-BR-03: Deposits by Branch

### Definition

Total deposit balances by branch.

### Formula

```sql
SUM(current_balance)
GROUP BY branch
```

### Business Purpose

Measures funding contribution.

---

## KPI-BR-04: Customer Growth by Branch

### Definition

Growth in customer count by branch.

### Formula

```text
Current Customers
-
Previous Customers
```

### Business Purpose

Measures branch expansion.

---

## KPI-BR-05: NPL Ratio by Branch

### Definition

Branch-level non-performing loan ratio.

### Formula

```text
Branch NPL
/
Branch Loan Portfolio × 100
```

### Business Purpose

Compares risk performance across branches.

# 8. KPI Governance

To ensure consistency:

* KPI formulas must remain standardized.
* KPI definitions must be documented.
* All dashboards must use approved KPI calculations.
* KPI calculations should be traceable to source data.

---

# 9. KPI Framework Summary

The KPI Framework establishes a consistent measurement system for evaluating business performance, customer engagement, branch productivity, and credit risk exposure.

These KPIs serve as the foundation for analytical reporting, SQL analysis, Power BI dashboards, and strategic decision-making throughout the Banking Risk & Business Intelligence Platform.
